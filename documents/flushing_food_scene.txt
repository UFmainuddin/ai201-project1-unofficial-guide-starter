Description: Flushing food scene: restaurants, food courts, cuisines, student budget options, cultural diversity
---
Flushing, Queens is widely considered the best Chinatown in New York City and one of the best in the United States. The food scene is exceptional and very affordable, making it ideal for students on a tight budget.

Key food destinations:

New World Mall Food Court (136-20 Roosevelt Ave, basement level): This underground food court has over 30 vendors serving Chinese regional cuisines including Sichuan, Shanghainese, Cantonese, and Fujianese food. Meals typically cost $5-10. It is one of the most popular food destinations for locals and tourists alike. The quality is very high relative to price.

Golden Shopping Mall (41-28 Main Street, basement level): Another underground food court with Lanzhou-style hand-pulled noodles, dumplings, and Xian-style street food. Very cheap meals starting at $4-6. Known especially for the lamb skewers and hand-pulled noodle dishes.

Main Street dining: Dozens of restaurants line Main Street serving Taiwanese bubble tea, Cantonese seafood, hot pot, dim sum, Korean BBQ, and South Asian food. The block between Roosevelt Avenue and 41st Avenue is especially dense with food options.

Union Street Korean food: The stretch of Union Street near Northern Boulevard has a concentration of Korean restaurants, bakeries, and grocery stores serving the large Korean community in Flushing.

Indian and South Asian food: A growing number of South Asian restaurants near Main Street and in the Kew Gardens Hills area (close to Queens College) serve halal Indian, Bangladeshi, and Pakistani food at affordable prices.

Grocery shopping: H Mart (large Korean supermarket) on Main Street, Hong Kong Supermarket, and multiple Asian grocery stores offer fresh produce and ingredients at prices much lower than Manhattan. Renting near Flushing means very affordable grocery options.

For students on a budget: It is realistically possible to eat full meals for $5-8 per meal near Flushing Main Street, every day. This is significantly cheaper than anywhere in Manhattan or most of Brooklyn.
